· Show: · Instant save for teachers: Open the inbox workbook ↗
How it works: when instant save is ON, a teacher's attendance save goes into the inbox and she gets “saved” at once. Every minute the writer job copies the inbox into the month workbook, one section at a time. Waiting = in the inbox, not yet in the sheet (the teacher already sees her marks in the app). Written = now in the sheet. Problems = could not be written after 3 tries (kept here so nothing is lost; enter them by hand). When it is OFF, saves go straight to the sheets as before and nothing waits here.
