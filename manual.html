1Sign in and install

Use the same username and password as the college portal. Sign in once with internet; after that the app works without internet too.

  1. Open the app link on your phone (Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone) or laptop.
  2. Type your Username and Password and tap Sign in.
  3. Install it like an app so it opens from the home screen:
    • Android (Chrome): menu ⋮ → Install app or Add to Home screen.
    • iPhone (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen.
    • Laptop (Chrome / Edge): the install icon at the right of the address bar.
  4. Keep the internet on for a minute the first time: your classes and months download to the phone.
Not sure what a button does? Tap the ? at the top and press Turn on hint mode, then tap any button to read what it does.

2Home screen Teacher

Home shows today's classes, test entry, student search and your sections at a glance. The three tabs at the top move between Home, Attendance and Reports.

Today

Your classes of today in period order, with the period, time and room from the college timetable. Mark opens the attendance of that class; a tick means today is already marked. The class going on now is marked Now, the next one Next.

Tests & exams

One card with three tabs: Monthly test (choose the month chip, then tap a class), Grand test and Detention. Grand test and Detention stay locked until the admin opens them (Grand test from 1 January, Detention from 1 February) and close at the deadline. A dot on a tab means it is open now.

My sections this month

Attendance of each class you teach this month, with a bar and the number of students below 73%. Tap a class for its summary.

Below these: Student search (see 9), Students reported to Chief Proctor, My timetable and, for admin/HOD/Chief Proctor, Free teachers.

The app follows your phone's light or dark setting automatically. The colours are the Jinnah uniform colours: brown, tea pink and white. Each card has its own colour on top, so you can find it quickly.

3Mark attendance Teacher

The screen you use every day.

  1. Choose class, subject and month in the brown card at the top. The line under it shows that day's period, time, theory/practical and room.
  2. Tap the day in the day strip (today is already selected). A green dot = saved, orange dot = a past day not marked, yellow dot = the day has a note, ×2 = two lectures.
  3. Mark each student: P present, A absent, L approved leave, B bunk (in college but missed the lecture). Tap the same letter again to clear it.
  4. Tip: tick Mark all P first, then change only the absentees.
  5. Press Save attendance at the bottom. The button shows how many students are still unmarked; everyone must be marked before you can save.
Only P counts as attended. A lecture counts as held when at least one student is P or A. Students on approved leave (from the leave in-charge's sheet) are already marked L with a pink label; you can still change it.

Search box and filters: type a name or roll no.; Unmarked shows only students you have not marked, Absent and Low (below 73%) the others.

Grey, struck-through students have left the subject or the college. They cannot be marked and are not counted.

Guest students (from another section) have a purple Guest tag. A guest is counted only against the lectures she was marked in.

Absentees PDF (bottom right) makes a list of today's absent, leave and bunk students with their attendance so far, ready to share on WhatsApp.

After saving, the status chip at the top right shows Synced when the marks have reached the sheet. Without internet it shows Offline or 3 to sync; the marks wait safely on the phone and go to the sheet by themselves later.

Saved a wrong mark? Open the same day (and lecture), correct it and save again. It overwrites.

6Monthly test Teacher

  1. On Home, open Tests & exams → Monthly test, choose the month chip (previous months can be opened and corrected) and tap the class.
  2. Enter the Total marks first (for example 10 or 20).
  3. Type each student's marks, or A for absent. Marks above the total are refused. Fill empty with A marks all empty rows absent (Undo is offered).
  4. Save test marks. Every student must have marks or A.
Marks go to the M.T column of the attendance sheet and into the real "<Month> - MONTHLY TEST" workbook (Obtained Marks of your subject). Marks entered in the old portal also show here.

7Grand test Teacher

Open Tests & exams → Grand test → Open Grand test. The button is locked until the admin opens the grand test (1 January by default) and locks again after the deadline.

  1. Tap one of your sections/subjects (only yours are listed).
  2. The total marks are fixed by the admin for the whole college (for example 40); teachers cannot change them.
  3. Enter marks, A (absent) or L (leave) for every student. Save stays disabled until every entry is filled.
  4. Award list PDF makes the award list of that subject.
You can work offline: entries wait on the phone and are sent when internet returns.

🎙Voice: speak instead of typing, listen to the manual

The app can listen and speak. Listening (speech to text) needs internet and permission for the microphone; reading aloud works offline. Best in Chrome on Android, Chrome/Edge on a laptop and Safari on iPhone.

Voice typing

Tap in any text box (search, comments, notes, marks). A round brown 🎙 button appears at the bottom right. Tap it and speak; tap again to stop. The small button next to it switches between English and اردو. In a marks box, “seven”, “8.5” or “absent” become 7, 8.5 or A.

Voice roll call

  1. Open a class and day, then ⋯ More → Voice roll call.
  2. The app says each student’s name and the row turns highlighted.
  3. Answer present (yes, haazir, ji), absent (no, ghair haazir), leave (chutti) or bunk.
  4. Say next to skip, back for the previous student, repeat, or stop. You can tap P A L B in the window at any time.
  5. At the end the app tells the totals. Check the list and press Save attendance as usual. Nothing is saved by voice alone.

The microphone stays on during the whole roll call, so you can answer one after another without waiting. While the app reads a name it ignores the microphone, so answer after the name. Options in the window: Read names aloud (turn it off and read the names yourself for the fastest roll call), Only unmarked, and the answer language.

Say the absentees

Fastest for a big class: ⋯ More → Say the absentees, then say “absent 3, 7 and 12, leave 5” using the row numbers on the left (first names also work). The window lists what it understood; press Apply, and everyone not named becomes P. Then Save.

Speak marks (monthly test)

On the monthly test screen, enter the total, then tap 🎙 Speak marks next to “Fill empty with A”. The app says each name; say the marks or “absent”. Marks above the total are refused and asked again. Press Save test marks when done.

Listen to the manual

In the app, the ? help window has 🔊 Listen: it reads the help from where you are. In this manual, every chapter has a 🔊 button, and the bar at the top has Listen to all and the reading speed.

Voice settings and a natural voice

⋯ More → Voice settings (also ⚙ Voice in the help window and in every voice window): choose the English and the Urdu voice, whether the app speaks English or اردو, and the speed. ★ marks the most natural voice found on the device. Test microphone shows a level bar and what the app heard.

  • Laptop: Microsoft Edge has the most natural voices (“Online (Natural)”, also Urdu: Uzma and Asad). Chrome has “Google UK English Female”.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Text-to-speech (or Accessibility → Text-to-speech) → Speech services by Google → install voice data for English (India) and Urdu.
  • iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → download an Enhanced or Premium voice.
If it does not listen: use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (Firefox, Brave and Opera cannot listen); allow the microphone (lock icon beside the address → Microphone → Allow); on Windows also Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone → allow the browser, and pick the right microphone in Sound settings; check the internet. A headset microphone works best in a noisy class. Voice settings → Test microphone tells you which of these is the problem.

20AAdmission rules: the complete rulebook Admin

Every rule the app (Admissions page) and the New Session List script follow when they decide who gets which seat, which fee is right and who must be shifted. The same rules are used everywhere: merit lists, seat counts, From portal recommendations, refund list, shifting letter and the Excel list of the New Session List script.

  • Only Pre-Medical has subsidized and non-subsidized seats. So only Pre-Medical students can be shifted (S ↔ M) or get a half-fee refund. F.A, FCS and Pre-Engineering have one fee for everyone.
  • The ladder is Pre-Medical (S) → Self Morning (M) → Evening (E). Every Pre-Medical (S) quota takes its students from all Pre-Medical programs: the best students of that quota get the subsidized seats, wherever they were admitted.
  • The fees shown on each merit list card are the most common paid challan of that program (e.g. Rs 60,000 and Rs 120,000).

2. Aggregate

Aggregate = (Obtained marks + 20 if Hafiz-e-Quran) × 100 ÷ Total marks, 2 decimals. HQ (Hafiz-e-Quran) comes from the portal. Example: 1080/1200 with HQ = (1080 + 20) × 100 ÷ 1200 = 91.67.
  • A student with no matric marks on the portal has no Aggregate, so she has no merit place until her marks are filled.
  • O-Level students have their own totals (e.g. 905/1100); the Aggregate makes them comparable.

3. MeritNo and Age-wise

  • MeritNo is counted separately inside every program and every quota (1st Year only). Highest Aggregate = 1.
  • Equal Aggregate = the same MeritNo, and no number is skipped (1, 2, 2, 2, 3 …).
  • Age-wise ranks only the students who share an Aggregate: the older one first (from DOB) gets “1st”, then “2nd” … Students with a unique Aggregate have it empty.
  • So the order of a merit list is: Aggregate (high to low) → older first.

5. Last date of admission and late students

  • Set in Merit lists → Last date of admission → Save date (for every device). Empty date = no late rule.
  • A student whose FEE PAID DATE is after the last date, or who has not paid, is late. Her row says “Late: fee paid …, after the last date” or “Fee not paid by the last date”.
  • Seats are given first to on-time students in merit order. Late students come after every on-time student of the quota, by Aggregate, then the earlier fee date, then the older first.
  • So the late rule matters only when there are more students than seats. If a seat is still empty after all on-time students, a late student gets it.
  • Bank forwarded the fee late: if she paid in time but the portal shows a later date, press ✎ next to Fee paid in her merit list row and enter the date on her paid receipt and why (e.g. “Receipt seen, bank forwarded late”). It is kept in the strength sheet (FEE RECEIPT DATE, FEE DATE NOTE); portal syncs never change it, and every merit list uses it instead of the portal date. Remove goes back to the portal date.

6. Seats

  • Seats of every program and quota are set in Admissions → Seat settings (saved in the Quota Settings sheet). Every change is written in Seat Changes with who and when.
  • Own seats: e.g. Pre-Medical (S) Open Quota 85, O-Level Quota 6, F.A Afghan Quota 5.
  • Shared seats (pools): one number of seats that several programs fill together, with one merit list for all of them. E.g. Afghan Quota shared by Pre Medical Open, Pre Engg, FCS; Sports Quota shared by FA, FCS, Pre Engg, Pre Medical Open. The pool's merit list and its refund list are built under Pre-Medical (S) when Pre-Medical is in the pool.
  • A quota with no seats set has no seat limit (e.g. UoP Employee Quota: every employee's daughter is admitted on a subsidized seat).
  • Evening Pre-Medical admitting this year (Merit lists tab): on = Self Morning seats are a limit and the rest go to Evening; off = Self Morning takes everybody.
  • Surplus (more students than seats) is allowed only in Open Quota of non-subsidized Pre-Medical, FCS, F.A and Pre-Engineering. Anywhere else it is shown as a problem.
  • Students who left, cancelled or migrated out are not counted: their seat becomes empty for the next student.

7. Who gets a seat (the order)

  1. All students of the quota (for Pre-Medical (S): from every Pre-Medical program) are put in order: on-time first, then Aggregate high to low, then older first; late students after them.
  2. The first seats students get the seats. A thick line marks the last seat.
  3. Below the line: Next in line (when a subsidized seat empties she moves up), and Possible candidate (yellow) for an empty seat of this list.
  4. A student above the line who is not in the subsidized program = wrong place: she should move up to subsidized (shifting letter + half-fee refund). A student in the subsidized program below the line should move down to Self Morning (M).
  5. Ties at the last seat are decided by age (older first). Example: if 85 seats are full, the 86th student goes to Self Morning even if her Aggregate equals the 85th.

9. Migrated students

  • 1st Year migrated in: students on the board's (BISEP) migration list; paste the list in Merit lists → Paste the BISEP migration list (the UOP portal does not tell). Matched by name and father name.
  • 2nd Year migrated in: on the BISEP list, or no 1st-year payment on the UOP portal (first fee on or after 1 August of this session), or Enroll# in the migration range, or listed in the sheet “Migrated to JCW”. The list's last column says why.
  • Migrated-in students are not placed in the 1st Year merit lists.

10. Half-fee refund list and shifting letter

  • Who: Pre-Medical students on a subsidized seat who paid the non-subsidized fee. Refund = what she paid − subsidized fee (e.g. 120,000 − 60,000 = 60,000).
  • Tick Also the other subsidized quotas to include Minority, O-Level, Sports, Afghan … in the same list or letter.
  • Order: each quota together — Open Quota first, then the other quotas A–Z (O-Level, Sports …); highest Aggregate first inside each quota.
  • HQ cases: when her matric marks alone are below the last seat but HQ +20 puts her on a seat, the refund list adds Obtained, HQ, Aggregate columns, and the letter writes e.g. 1080/1200 + 20 HQ, Aggregate 91.67%.
  • In the letter the quota is written after the marks when it is not Open Quota, e.g. 1045/1200 (Sports Quota).
  • Continuation letter: for students found after a letter was already sent. Only students not in an earlier letter are listed, and the letter begins “In continuation of this office letter No. … dated …”. See 20B.
